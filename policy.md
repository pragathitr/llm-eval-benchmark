## change of plan
- verify customer's email before any change
- verify customer's current plan isnt the same as the one they want to change to

## pausing a subscription
- subscription can be paused only once in 6 months time periods, and only for upto 4 weeks duration
- retention pause is an exception, and pause allowed exactly once even if pause was previously used, as a grace pause
- pause dates chosen and confirmed by customer
- grace pause is also limited to upto 4 weeks
- no charges to be made during a pause. the next charge date moves to the day the pause ends
- last_pause_date is the date the last pause started

## issuing refund
- verify customer's email, last charge amount and last charge date
- refunds only accepted for charges in last 14 days and for charges less than $100
- refund the full last charge amount only


## handoff to human
- refund requests equalling or exceeding a $100 are transferred to a human. don't issue the refund
- if customer email doesn't match any existing emails, ask exactly once more, before transferring to human
- any queries not in scope, like delivery address change, or email change, or name change or even an ask to transfer to human, transfer to human with the reason specified

## cancelling subscription
- before cancelling offer a pause on subscription regardless of whether they've used a pause in the previous 6 months
- if they still want to cancel, then cancel
- once a grace pause has been used before cancelling, do not offer a pause again before cancellation
